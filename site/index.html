READ THE BOUNDARY OF THE CLAIM

Ten classes are not the whole representation.

The original model is DINOv3 plus a fixed patch classifier. A ten-class softmax constrains at most nine independent logit contrasts per image. Low KL can coexist with lost geometry or information. These results measure this readout's fidelity, not universal DINO preservation or concept discovery.
